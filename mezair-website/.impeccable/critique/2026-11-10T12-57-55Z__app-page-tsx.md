## Overall Impression

Honest, tidy, and inert. It reads as a careful index of what exists, written to avoid overclaiming, with nothing a visitor would repeat to a colleague. The biggest opportunity: lead with a claim and a picture that only Mezair can make, and cut the page by more than half.

## What's Working

- Honesty is a real trust asset: real captures, stated limits. Keep the facts, move them out of the pitch's way.
- The identity is ownable: mark, display type, restrained orange, no gradient text or glass.
- The raw material is concrete and true ("undo a turn from the thread", "approve from Apple Watch", "labelled issue to confirmed merge"). It is in the wrong place at the wrong size.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The first viewport does not say what Mezair is or why to switch.** The H1 carries no information. The lede's first clause is the pitch and by sentence four it is a pricing footnote for a paid add-on the visitor has not met. Eight links compete with the download. Fix: make the H1 the claim, one short supporting line, "free and open source" as the only price fact, one primary action. Command: /impeccable clarify.

**[P0] The product is below the fold and the image proves table stakes.** One agent, one thread, a toy task. Fix: a full app window at the fold showing what only Mezair has, then two or three more real captures (factory line, Mesh job, review surface) each carrying one claim. Command: /impeccable bolder.

**[P1] Process framing instead of outcome.** "Describe the task / Let the agent work / Review the change" is the mechanism every agent tool has shared since 2024. Nothing says what the user gets. Fix: delete the step strip; pair outcome claims with captures. Command: /impeccable clarify.

**[P1] Order and repetition bury the pitch.** £8.99 is stated 3 times, the provider list 3 times, open source 7 times, no-sign-up twice. The paid add-on is section two. Three screens go to shortcuts, unrelated tools and clone commands. Fix: hero, three or four differentiators with captures, one trust band, a small Sync band, close. Move other tools and the clone block out. Command: /impeccable distill.

**[P2] The download moment is fragile and the close is limp.** arm64 .dmg direct, unsigned warning in fine print, "Download for Mac" shown to phone and Windows visitors, and the close gives no reason to act. Fix: label the build, show the right platform, restate the strongest claim with the app image at the close. Command: /impeccable harden.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** cannot tell from the first screen whether this is an editor, a CLI wrapper or a cloud service. Meets "Sync & Mesh", "self-host", "unsigned" and "pronounced may-ZAIR" before any picture. "Bring your own subscriptions" is never explained; the answer (no API key, no margin) exists in the pricing FAQ and not here.

**Casey (mobile):** first viewport is mark, H1, lede and a Mac download button they cannot use; the consent banner covers the bottom 40%. The product arrives after 1.2 screens; the page is 10.2 screens of single-column text. The git clone block clips mid-URL.

**Alex (impatient power user):** "why this over Conductor, the Codex app or Claude Code desktop?" is never answered. No parallel-agent image, no worktree visual, no version, date or changelog. The demo is a toy. The shortcut table is filler.

## Minor Observations

- The Sync section lights an empty stage: a glowing void between heading and copy.
- Headings are labels ("What else is in the app", "Other Mezair tools"). Uniform descriptive sentence-case headings and three-item lists in every row are most of why it reads as machine-written.
- "No account. Everything stays in a local database." is a headline-grade claim filed under keyboard shortcuts.
- The footer tagline is clearer than the H1.
- The provider strip is grey text; OpenAI, Azure OpenAI and LLMGateway dilute the four names people recognise.
- Orange is reused for "How Sync & Mesh work", so the accent stops meaning download.
- The capture shows "1 failed" and "Review the failed command before relying on the result" in the hero image.
